You are helping build a historically valid benchmark-to-O*NET ability mapping.

By benchmark or evaluation, I mean a publicly documented dataset, task suite, leaderboard,
challenge, or standardized evaluation used to measure AI or robotic system performance, with
a defined task format and scoring metric. This may include text, vision, audio, speech,
multimodal, coding, web-use, robotics, or embodied-action evaluations, depending on the
O*NET ability.

# Task

For the O*NET ability {ability_name} in {target_year}, identify the best publicly available
benchmark or evaluation that existed by December 31 of {target_year}.

If no benchmark adequately measures this ability for example, the ability is primarily
physical, sensory, or interpersonal in a way that current AI evaluations do not meaningfully
test return "no adequate benchmark" rather than reaching for the closest available proxy.
Forcing a poor mapping is worse than acknowledging the gap.

O*NET ability:
- Name: {ability_name}
- Definition: {ability_definition}

  MATH = 3, GSM8K = 1 (grade school only), MMLU math = 2, MiniF2F = 2 (formal proofs only)
