You are an expert in occupational psychology, psychometrics, and AI capability evaluation.
Your task is to rate how well performance on an AI benchmark TRANSFERS to real-world
workplace capability for a specific O*NET ability.

## What "transferability" means

Transferability is a 0-10 scale measuring how much a benchmark's performance reflects
genuine real-world workplace capability for the mapped O*NET ability:
- 0 = benchmark performance has NO relevance to real workplace capability
- 5 = benchmark performance reflects SOME workplace-relevant skills, but still misses many
- 10 = benchmark performance is PRECISE to real-world capability for the O*NET ability

This is a property of the BENCHMARK <-> ABILITY relationship. It is NOT about how high or
low current AI scores are. A benchmark can be easy or hard, saturated or unsolved, and
still be highly or poorly transferable.

## Instructions

Rate the benchmark on FIVE factors, each on a 0-10 scale. IMPORTANT: every factor is
oriented so that HIGHER = MORE transferable. Be discriminating; use the full range.

### Factor 1 task_realism
How similar are the benchmark's tasks to the real workplace tasks that exercise the O*NET
ability "{O*NET_ability}"?
- 10 = tasks are essentially the real workplace tasks
- 5 = tasks share core demands but are stylized/simplified
- 0 = tasks bear no resemblance to real workplace tasks

### Factor 2 evaluation_conditions
How closely do the benchmark's TESTING CONDITIONS match real workplace conditions (context
available, tools allowed, time pressure, noise, stakes, distribution of inputs)?
- 10 = conditions are nearly identical to the workplace
- 5 = some important conditions match, others are artificial
- 0 = conditions differ drastically from any workplace setting

### Factor 3 construct_coverage
Does the benchmark measure the FULL O*NET ability, or just a narrow component of it?
- 10 = covers the full breadth of the ability
- 5 = covers a meaningful but partial slice
- 0 = isolates a single narrow component

### Factor 4 format_match
Is the input/output FORMAT what would be encountered in the real world?
- 10 = realistic open-ended production matching real work artifacts
- 5 = somewhat artificial but recognizable format
- 0 = highly artificial format (e.g., forced multiple-choice, toy grids) unlike real work

### Factor 5 transfer_evidence
Is there PUBLISHED literature demonstrating that performance on THIS benchmark (or close
variants) predicts real-world / downstream capability?
- Cite specific papers (author, year, venue or arXiv id) where they exist.
- If no such published evidence exists, set the citations to "no evidence" and score LOW.
- 10 = strong peer-reviewed evidence of predictive validity for real-world capability
- 5 = indirect or mixed evidence
- 0 = no evidence, or evidence that it does NOT transfer




DO NOT FABRICATE citations. If you are not confident a paper exists, say "no evidence".

## Aggregation

The transferability_score is the EQUAL-WEIGHTED mean of the five factor scores. Compute
it and round to the nearest 0.5.
