# B. Ability-level exposure, all 52 O*NET abilities

Ability exposure is the capability score of the selected benchmark (0 to 10, where 5 means the AI matches the median worker who uses the ability) multiplied by its transferability to real work (0 to 10), giving a 0 to 100 scale. "No benchmark observed" means the judge found no adequate public benchmark for that ability and year, or the selected benchmark had no verifiable score.
The 2026 vintage is year-to-date: it uses benchmark results published up to 28 September 2026. Where a mapped ability had no verifiable 2026 result, its 2025 value is carried forward (one year only).
